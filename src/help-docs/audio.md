## Audio Bible Player

### What it does

Use the audio footer to listen to Bible chapters while you read. The player supports choosing an audio author/version, chapter navigation, loop playback, and import/delete controls.

### Controls

- **Author / Version**: pick the audio package you want to use from the dropdown.
- **Play / Pause**: tap the round center button to start or stop the current chapter.
- **Previous / Next**: move chapter by chapter.
- **Progress slider**: scrub the audio position manually.
- **Scroll**: enable auto-scroll to keep the reader view synced with playback.
- **Loop**: cycle between `Off`, `Chapter`, and `Book` modes.

### Android-only features

- **Sync / UnSync**: when sync is active, the audio follows the current reader chapter. Tap again to unlock and play audio independently from the text.

### Importing audio

- Open the audio menu and choose **Import Zip** to add a new audio package.
- Use **Delete Author** to remove the currently selected audio author/version.

### Notes

- If no audio package is available, the audio controls will remain disabled until an author is imported.
- Audio files are loaded from the app's local audio storage, so the player works offline once imported.
