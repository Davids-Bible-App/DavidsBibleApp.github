## A Small Personal History

I started building DBA in 2023 after struggling to find a Bible app that worked
well offline on both my Windows desktop and Android phone without requiring a
cloud account or constant internet connection.

Most apps either lacked the translations I wanted, didn't support audio, or were
cluttered with social features I had no use for. DBA is my answer to that — a
quiet, focused reading environment.
