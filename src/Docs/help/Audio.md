## Audio Bible Player

### What it does

Use the audio footer to listen to Bible chapters while you read. The player supports choosing an audio author/version, chapter navigation, loop playback, independant playback from reader via sync toggle, and import/delete Authors(Audio-Bibles).

Opening the Audio Tray fully, reveals a Timer System Countdown to Pause the Audio, after your specified time period.

### Controls

- **Author / Version**: pick the audio package you want to use from the dropdown.
- **Play / Pause**: tap the round center button to start or stop the current chapter.
- **Previous / Next**: move chapter by chapter.
- **Progress slider**: scrub the audio position manually.
- **Scroll**: enable auto-scroll to keep the reader view synced with playback.
- **Loop**: cycle between `Off`, `Chapter`, and `Book` modes.
- **Sync'd / DeSync'd**: when sync is active, the audio follows the current reader chapter. Tap again to unlock and play audio independently from the text, allowing full App imersion without disturbing the Audio, Great for study, lookups, etc.
- **Timer:** Adjust the Hours and Minutes, Press Start, watch it countdown.

### Importing audio

- Open the audio menu and choose **Import Zip** to add a new audio package.
- Use **Delete Author** to remove the currently selected audio author/version.

### Notes

- _If no audio package is available, the audio controls will remain disabled until an author is imported._
- _Audio files are loaded from the app's local audio storage, so the player works offline once imported._
